My favourite build

Arduino Smart Backpack

I wanted to build something that actually does something useful. So I made a backpack with GPS tracking, GSM connectivity, a temperature sensor, and an SOS alert - all running on Arduino. It took a lot of debugging, a few burnt components, and way too many late nights. Worth it.

Academic work

"The Cost of Lies"

Valery Legasov's monologue - Chernobyl (HBO)

I analysed the rhetoric and ethics in Legasov's monologue from the Chernobyl miniseries. It turned into a deep dive on how institutional dishonesty makes disasters worse-and why one person telling the truth actually matters. Probably the most interesting thing I've written.

Communication Rhetoric Ethics Crisis Management
