Things I've worked on

Arduino Smart Backpack

I wanted to build something that actually does something useful. So I made a backpack with GPS tracking, GSM connectivity, a temperature sensor, and an SOS alert - all running on Arduino. It took a lot of debugging, a few burnt components, and way too many late nights. Worth it.

"The Cost of Lies" - Academic Presentation

I analysed the rhetoric and ethics in Valery Legasov's monologue from the Chernobyl (HBO) miniseries. It turned into a deep dive on how institutional dishonesty makes disasters worse - and why one person telling the truth actually matters. Probably the most interesting thing I've written.
